### Streamlining the AI Agent Workflow

To keep shipping daily without getting bogged down by repetitive manual setups, I optimized the AI-assisted writing pipeline for this blog today.

Instead of re-explaining tone and formatting guidelines for every post, I set up a structured system using the IDE's customization schema:
* **Workspace Rules (`AGENTS.md`):** Defines global constraints—my persona, tone, style rules, and vocabulary choices so the AI never adds unwanted filler or alters my voice.
* **Skill Playbooks (`SKILL.md`):** Procedural step-by-step guides that trigger on specific tasks (like taking raw notes from `draft/` and outputting clean markdown to `publish/`).

Separating rules (what to do) from skills (how to do it) keeps the agent workflow modular, reliable, and fast to iterate on.
