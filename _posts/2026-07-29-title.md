### Brainstorming My Master's Thesis: World Models

For the past two days, I've shifted my focus from coding to research, brainstorming titles and proposals for my Master’s research project. My research is centered on frontier world models—a topic I'm incredibly passionate about.

As an AI scientist, I find Yann LeCun's work on Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) highly compelling. I believe true machine intelligence and agency will be built on world models that learn latent representations of the environment to predict the next physical state. This is fundamentally different from current generative autoregressive LLMs, which are limited to predicting the next token or action.

LeCun's latest work, LeWorldModel, is particularly impressive in how it comprehends complex physical AI scenarios, though it still faces challenges with long-horizon planning tasks. 

I’ve finalized my project proposal and title, and I'm currently waiting for my supervisor's approval to move the needle on it.
